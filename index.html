KharchIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

KharchIQ-Plan & Track ("KharchIQ", "the app", "we", "our", or "us") is a personal finance application designed to help users manage and understand their personal financial information.

This Privacy Policy explains how KharchIQ handles information when you use the app.

1. Information stored in KharchIQ

KharchIQ allows you to enter and manage financial information such as:

  • Income records
  • Expense records
  • Account information
  • Account balances
  • Transfers
  • Budgets and budget settings
  • Bills and subscriptions
  • Payment history
  • Credit card information entered into the app
  • Categories and payment methods
  • App settings and preferences

This information is used to provide the financial management features of KharchIQ.

2. Where your financial data is stored

KharchIQ is designed to keep your financial data on your device.

KharchIQ does not operate a cloud server or account-based backend for storing your financial records. The app does not routinely transmit your financial records to KharchIQ or to an external analytics or advertising service.

Your financial information is therefore primarily stored locally on the device where you use KharchIQ.

3. Information we collect

KharchIQ does not collect personal financial information on a KharchIQ-operated server.

The current version of the app does not use:

  • Advertising tracking
  • Third-party analytics services
  • User behavior tracking
  • A KharchIQ online account system
  • A KharchIQ cloud database for financial records
  • Location tracking
  • Contact-list collection
  • Camera or microphone collection

KharchIQ does not require you to create an online account to use its core financial-management features.

4. App Lock and PIN

KharchIQ provides an optional App Lock using a PIN.

Your PIN itself is not stored as plain text. KharchIQ creates a salted verifier and stores that verifier using secure storage provided by the application.

KharchIQ does not store or have access to your device's biometric fingerprint, face data, or biometric templates.

When biometric authentication is used, authentication is performed through the device's supported biometric authentication system.

5. Notifications

KharchIQ can create local notifications for features such as reminders.

These notifications are generated on the device using information already available to the app. KharchIQ does not use a remote notification server to routinely transmit your financial information for these notifications.

6. Backup and restore

KharchIQ provides manual and automatic backup functionality.

Backup data can contain the financial information stored by the app, including settings, transactions, accounts, budgets, bills, subscriptions, and related records.

Automatic backups are stored locally according to the app's backup functionality.

When you use the app's sharing functionality to share a backup, the backup is transferred through the Android sharing system to the destination you select. The destination application or service may have its own privacy policy and terms. KharchIQ does not control how a third-party destination handles a backup after you choose to share it.

You should only share backup files with destinations you trust.

7. Third-party services and libraries

KharchIQ uses Android and Capacitor components and libraries to provide functions such as:

  • Android application functionality
  • Local notifications
  • Secure biometric authentication
  • Secure storage
  • Local file storage
  • Android file sharing

These components are used to provide the app's functionality. KharchIQ does not use advertising or analytics SDKs to build advertising profiles or track users.

Third-party operating-system components and services may process information according to their own policies.

8. Data sharing

KharchIQ does not sell your personal or financial information.

KharchIQ does not routinely share your financial records with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

If you explicitly use a device sharing function to send a backup or other file to another application or service, that transfer is initiated by you and is subject to the recipient application's privacy practices.

9. Data security

KharchIQ uses reasonable technical measures appropriate to the functions provided by the application.

For App Lock, PIN verification uses a salted verifier rather than storing the PIN as plain text, and sensitive authentication functionality uses Android secure authentication mechanisms where applicable.

However, no software or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for keeping exported or shared backup files secure.

10. Data deletion

KharchIQ does not maintain a KharchIQ cloud account containing your financial records.

You can delete financial records and other locally stored app data using the functionality provided by KharchIQ or by removing the application's local data through your Android device.

Deleting the application's local data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored financial information that has not been backed up.

Backup files that you have manually saved or shared outside the app may remain in the location or service where you saved or shared them. Those files must be deleted separately from their respective locations.

11. Children's privacy

KharchIQ is a general personal finance application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a KharchIQ-operated online service.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KharchIQ's functionality or privacy practices change.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this policy will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
